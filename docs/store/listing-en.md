## App name (max 30)

TrackFlow: Habit Tracker

## Short description (max 80)

Offline habit tracker: one-tap check-ins, streaks and a 365-day progress map.

## Full description (max 4000)

TrackFlow is a fast, private habit tracker. No account, no ads, no internet: your habits stay on your phone.

TODAY'S FLOW
• Group habits by time of day: morning, deep work, evening or anytime.
• Yes/no habits complete with one tap.
• Measurable habits (glasses of water, pages, minutes) have a quick +/- stepper and a progress bar.
• Every card shows the last seven days; tap any day to fix a missed check-in.

7-DAY MATRIX
• See all your habits against the last seven days in one table.
• Month-to-date consistency, a rolling 30-day rate, perfect days and your best streak.

365-DAY CANVAS
• A year of progress as a heatmap, for all habits or one habit.
• Rest days are shown differently from missed days. Tap any day to see what was done.

SCHEDULES THAT FIT YOUR WEEK
• Choose the weekdays a habit repeats on. Rest days never break a streak.

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Undo after deleting a habit.
• Copy a backup of all your habits as text and paste it back to restore. A restore always asks first and can be undone.

PRIVATE
• Everything is stored only on your phone. The app has no internet permission, no analytics and no tracking.
