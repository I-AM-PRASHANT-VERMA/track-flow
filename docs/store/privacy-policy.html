TrackFlow - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

TrackFlow is a habit tracker. This policy explains what the app stores, when anything leaves your phone, and who can see it.

What the app stores, and where

The habits you create and the progress you log are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. The app does not ask for an account and has no internet permission: it cannot send anything over the network. We, the developer, do not run a server, never receive your data and have no way to read it.

When data leaves your phone

  • Backups you copy. "Copy Backup" puts all your habits and their history, as plain text, on your phone's clipboard. The text is not encrypted. Where you paste it (for example a note or an email to yourself) is your choice, and that app's own privacy terms then apply.
  • Your phone's own backup. If your phone's Google backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your phone's encrypted backup in your Google account, so it comes back on a new phone. You control this in your phone's settings.

In both cases the data goes only where you choose. It is never sent to us.

No tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking libraries, and it does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. It uses vibration for tap feedback.

Deleting your data

Deleting a habit in the app removes it from the phone (you can undo for a few seconds). Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone. A backup you pasted somewhere else, or your phone's own Google backup, stays where it is until you delete it there.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
